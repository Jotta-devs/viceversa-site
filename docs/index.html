Timeline of the case

2022 → 2026
February 2022

Rockstar confirms the game exists

After years of rumors, the studio officially announces that the next Grand Theft Auto is in development. The internet was never the same.

September 2022

The mega-leak

Dozens of videos from unfinished builds hit the web, in what journalists described as one of the largest leaks in video game history.

December 2023

Trailer 1 — and the return to Vice City

The first trailer reveals Lucia, the state of Leonida and the return to Vice City. Published hours ahead of schedule after leaking on social media, it broke viewership records on day one.

May 2025

First delay + Trailer 2

The release moves from 2025 to May 26, 2026. Four days later, the second trailer properly introduces the Jason and Lucia duo — and calms fans down (a little).

November 2025

Second delay: the definitive date

Rockstar moves the release to November 19, 2026, saying it needs the extra months to deliver the level of polish the community expects.

June 2026

Pre-orders open and prices revealed

Prices announced, pre-orders live, editions detailed: $79.99 and $99.99.

August 6, 2026

Rockstar and Netflix announce the Extended Look

A first-of-its-kind partnership: the presentation would premiere on streaming six hours before reaching YouTube. No game had ever used a streaming platform as its primary marketing channel.

August 18–26, 2026

The wave of leaks — and Rockstar's letter

Gameplay videos start circulating almost daily from August 18. Take-Two secures court authorization to trace the source, and on the 26th Rockstar publishes an open letter regretting the episode and confirming the schedule still stands.

August 27, 2026 · today

The Extended Look airs

26 minutes of gameplay captured on a base PlayStation 5, premiering on Netflix and going free on YouTube six hours later. Reworked combat, Red Dead 2-style NPC interaction, the sixth wanted star and instant switching between the protagonists.

The Leonida files

Trivia

Bonnie & Clyde in the Florida heat

For the first time the series has a romantic pair of protagonists: Jason Duval e Lucia Caminos. Lucia is the first female protagonist of a 3D-era GTA — and the game opens with her leaving prison.

Leonida is Florida (barely disguised)

The fictional state of Leonida is inspired by present-day Florida: swamps, beaches, neon motels and, of course, Vice City — the series' Miami, absent from the main timeline for nearly two decades.

18 years away from Vice City

The last canonical visit to Vice City was in GTA: Vice City Stories (2006). The pre-order even leans on the nostalgia: the Vintage Pack brings classic items from the 2002 Vice City.

"Before GTA 6"

The wait became a unit of time. The meme "happened before GTA 6" came to mark any improbable real-world event — from elections to eclipses — that arrived before the game.

The whole industry waited for the date

Major publishers held back announcements and rescheduled releases to avoid the competition — one journalist called the phenomenon "4D chess" played by the entire games industry.

Record stolen (barely)

The first trailer broke viewership records in 2023 and only lost the crown in 2026 — to a Spider-Man movie trailer that passed 500 million views in 12 hours.

Frequently asked questions

FAQ
How do I watch the Extended Look?

It's already live on Netflix for subscribers, and at 9 PM ET today it becomes free on Rockstar's YouTube channel and the official GTA VI site. It runs 26 minutes. You don't need a pre-order or a Rockstar account to watch the public version.

What was revealed in the Extended Look?

Real gameplay captured on a base PS5 with Jason and Lucia: reworked combat, Red Dead 2-style NPC interaction, slim jim car theft, the return of the sixth wanted star, instant character switching when the pair are together, hints of a morality system and a long list of side activities — from scuba diving to dirt biking.

When does GTA VI come out?

On November 19, 2026, worldwide, for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, with pre-loading from November 12.

Is it coming to PC, PS4 or Xbox One?

PS4 and Xbox One will not get the game. The PC version has not been announced for launch; based on Rockstar's track record, it usually arrives 12 to 24 months after the consoles.

How much does it cost?

$79.99 for the standard edition and $99.99 for the Ultimate Edition, which includes the Vintage Vice City Pack and extra in-campaign perks. Pre-orders are already open.

Will there be an online mode at launch?

Rockstar confirmed that, at launch, GTA VI is a single-player experience centered on Jason and Lucia. Plans for multiplayer have not been detailed yet.
