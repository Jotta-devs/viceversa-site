These are the rules we follow when deciding what to publish and how.

Facts are facts, rumors are rumors

We always make clear where information comes from. Confirmations from Rockstar Games, Take-Two or people working on the game are treated as fact. Information from insiders, dataminers or unconfirmed reports is presented as rumor, with the source identified, and never stated as certain in a headline.

Sources

  • We prioritize official channels: Rockstar Games' website and social accounts, Take-Two announcements and reports, interviews with the game's team.
  • When a story comes from another outlet, we credit that outlet.
  • We don't publish information we can't attribute to a source.

Leaks

We report that a leak happened when it's newsworthy, but we do not publish or link to leaked material — code, builds, videos or internal files. We also warn readers when a supposed leak is fake or risky, such as files carrying malware.

Corrections

When we get something wrong, we fix it. When a factual error is found, we update the article and add a correction note at the end explaining what changed. Typo and formatting fixes don't get a note. To report an error, use the contact page.

Independence and advertising

VICEVERSA has no connection to Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. Ads on the site are served by Google and labeled as advertising. The ebook sold on the site is our own product and is always presented as such. No advertiser or partner decides what we publish.

Images

The skyline, palm trees and the map of Leonida on this site are our own illustrations. In news stories, third-party images — such as captures from official trailers — are used for informational purposes and credited when the source is known.
