This policy explains what data is collected when you visit vvviceversa.com, by whom, why, and how you can control it.

What VICEVERSA does not collect

The site has no sign-up, login, forms or comments. We don't ask for your name, email or any personal data, and we don't sell anyone's data.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how many people visit the site and which pages they read. It uses cookies and records data such as pages visited, device and browser type, approximate location and where the visit came from. This data reaches us in aggregate form and doesn't identify you. You can block Google Analytics with Google's opt-out browser add-on.

Google ads (AdSense)

The site shows ads provided by Google AdSense. Third parties, including Google, may place and read cookies on your browser, or use web beacons, IP addresses and other identifiers to collect information as a result of ads being served on this site. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites, to limit how often you see an ad and to prevent fraud, even when ads are not personalized. You can:

  • turn off personalized ads in Google's Ads Settings;
  • opt out of personalized ads from other vendors at aboutads.info;
  • learn more about how Google uses data from partner sites.

Consent and preferences

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland see Google's consent message, a consent management platform certified by Google. In those regions, advertising and Google Analytics cookies are only used after you consent, and you can change your choice at any time through the Privacy settings link in the footer of every page. Elsewhere, you can turn off personalized ads with the tools above or delete and block cookies in your browser.

Other services

  • Hosting: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs technical access data such as IP addresses for security.
  • Fonts: the site's fonts are served by Google Fonts, which receives your browser's IP address when loading them.
  • News images: some images are loaded from GitHub's servers.
  • Ebook purchases: payment happens entirely on Kiwify. We never receive or store your payment details.
  • External links: social networks and other sites we link to have their own policies.

Storage in your browser

We store a few choices in your own browser (local storage) so we don't ask again:

  • whether you accepted or dismissed the language suggestion shown at the top of the site;
  • on the map of Leonida, the places you marked as visited and the style you chose (Day or Night).

This information never leaves your device, doesn't identify you and can be erased by clearing the site's data in your browser.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, Brazil's LGPD and similar laws, you can request access to, correction of or deletion of personal data. Since VICEVERSA doesn't keep personal data of its own, requests about browsing and advertising data should go directly to Google through the tools above. You can also delete or block cookies in your browser settings at any time.

Children

GTA VI is a game for adults and this site is not directed at children.
