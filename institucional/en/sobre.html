VICEVERSA is a fan site dedicated to Grand Theft Auto VI. We bring together confirmed news, a timeline of the game since its first announcement, trivia about Leonida and an interactive map we drew ourselves from what Rockstar has officially shown.

What you'll find here

  • News — what was announced, what changed and what is still rumor, always clearly labeled.
  • Timeline — the key GTA VI milestones, from the reveal to launch.
  • Map of Leonida — an original, interactive illustration of the revealed regions.
  • Countdown to launch and answers to the most common questions.

Three languages

The site is published in English, Portuguese and Spanish. Some stories come out in English first and get translated shortly after.

Independence

VICEVERSA is a fan project with no connection to Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. We receive no inside information, early copies or payment from either company. Grand Theft Auto and related names are trademarks of their respective owners.

The site is supported by ads and by sales of our ebook. Neither influences what we publish — see our editorial policy.
